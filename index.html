THE WORKFLOW

From reference
to printable model.

01

Reference

Studying proportions, profiles, deck geometry and recognizable details from available references.

02

3D Design

Building the model in CAD with clean geometry, scale consistency and RC integration in mind.

03

Print Engineering

Splitting, tolerancing and refining parts so the digital model can become a practical physical build.

04

Test & Refine

Iterating assemblies, visual details and functional components for better real-world results.

R3D MARINE

Marine design for people who build.

R3D Marine creates detailed digital replicas, RC boat projects and 3D-printable marine components. The goal is simple: turn real-world marine design into models that look right, print well and are enjoyable to build.
